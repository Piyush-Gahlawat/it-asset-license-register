# IT Asset & License Management Guide

*This guide outlines the methodology for tracking IT hardware and software licenses to optimize costs, ensure compliance, and manage lifecycle replacements.*

## 1. Hardware Lifecycle Management
IT hardware (laptops, servers, networking gear) must be tracked from procurement to retirement. The standard lifecycle for a corporate laptop is 3 to 4 years.
- **Procurement:** Asset is tagged with a unique ID (e.g., AST-001).
- **Deployment:** Assigned to a specific user or department.
- **Maintenance:** Warranty tracked. Any repairs logged.
- **Retirement:** Wiped, data-destroyed, and disposed of or sold after year 4.

## 2. Software License Optimization (Avoiding Shelfware)
Paying for unused software licenses is a massive waste of IT budget. 
- **The 80% Rule:** If a software license utilization drops below 80%, the IT Manager must either reallocate the license to a new user or reduce the seat count at the next renewal.
- **Renewal Alert:** Contracts must be reviewed 90 days before the renewal date to allow time for negotiation or cancellation.

## 3. KPI Tracking
- **Hardware Warranty Expiry:** 0 devices should fall out of warranty without a replacement plan.
- **Software Utilization Rate:** Target is >90% across all active software subscriptions.

## 4. Roles & Responsibilities
- **IT Support / Helpdesk:** Updates the Hardware Register when devices are deployed or returned.
- **IT Manager:** Reviews the Software License Tracker quarterly to optimize spend and authorize renewals.
- **Procurement:** Handles vendor negotiations for software renewals based on the utilization data.

## 5. Audit Readiness
During an internal or external IT audit, the Asset Register is the primary evidence that the organization has control over its infrastructure. 
- Every device must have a unique Asset ID.
- Software must be reconcilable against purchased invoices.
- Retired devices must have a documented data destruction record.
